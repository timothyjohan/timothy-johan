## Palette
- **Canvas Base:** `#090d16` (deep slate/obsidian canvas)
- **Surface Elevated:** `#111827` (solid matte cards and containers)
- **Surface Border:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` / `#1f2937`

- **Secondary Text:** `#9ca3af` (neutral supporting copy, WCAG AA compliant)
- **Single Accent:** `#38bdf8` (Sky Cyan — reserved for key CTA, active links, and focal highlights)

## Typography
- **Font Family:** `Plus Jakarta Sans`, sans-serif
- **Headings:** Bold to extra-bold, tight tracking (`tracking-tight`), crisp contrast
- **Body:** Regular to medium, line-height 1.6 - 1.7 for optimal legibility

## Layout & Rhythm
- Varied compositions:
  - Hero with prominent portrait, clear value statement, and direct CTAs
  - About section with structured technical credentials and real story
  - Services section with accessible interactive flip cards with keyboard support
  - Tech stack grid with categorized badges
  - Featured projects with clean tags, descriptions, and verified working links
  - High-clarity contact area with direct action links

## Motion & Restraint
- Motion dial 1: Transitions on hover, click, and focus only.
- No perpetual loop animations (no endless gear rotations or infinite pulsing dots).
- Accessible focus outlines on all interactive elements.
